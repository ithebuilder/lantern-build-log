Lantern
Build log, updated as we go

A free, five-minute book summary that arrives in the part of your day you choose.

Lantern helps you decide which classic to read next. It is a small, free, personal project. The books are public domain. The summaries are AI-written and checked against the original text.

Building: sign-in and onboarding (slice 1 of 5)
Last updated 5 Oct 2026

How it will work

1 · Choose

Pick a book, or let us surprise you

Twelve classics to start, from Austen to Tagore. One summary at a time.

2 · Wait

It arrives in your window

Morning, midday, evening or wind-down, in your timezone. Never between 22:00 and 07:00.

3 · Read

Five minutes, then decide

Finish it on Telegram or the web, rate it, and see where to find the full book for free.

What we promise

  • Public domain only.Every title is clear under both Indian and US law before it enters the catalogue.
  • Honest about AI.Each summary says it is AI-written, and each one is checked against the source text by a person before it ships.
  • Private by design.One cookie, no trackers, no tracking pixels in email, pseudonymous analytics you consent to separately, delete your account any time.
  • Free, with no catch.No ads. Later, if you buy a paper copy through a link we show, we may earn a small commission, and we will say so on the page.
